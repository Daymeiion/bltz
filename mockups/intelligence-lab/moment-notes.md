# Selected Moment B: concise branded source inspection

## Boundary and audience

This refines the approved B source-inspection direction for BLTZ researchers and sports executives. The shared three-column workspace, athlete portrait/identity, search, themes, signals and opportunities remain root-owned. Only the center Moment renderer, its scoped stylesheet and this note are changed. This is an isolated prototype; no app route, database, provider, permission or onboarding change is made.

## Pass one: plan

The six shared brand colors are Night `#080d17`, Navy `#141e30`, Cloud `#f5f7fc`, Ink `#1a263b`, Gold `#ffbb00` and Blue `#287be5`. The renderer consumes shared theme tokens rather than writing its own hex colors. A restrained Oswald event heading provides BLTZ character; Geist body text carries the evidence; utility captions use the shared utility font. Rounded B geometry is retained in both light and dark themes.

The signature is a small evidence file that changes beneath a persistent athlete portrait. Performance appears once in the compact event header. Source inspection explains which record establishes those facts. Significance, score and the retrospective remain in the right rail.

## Pass two: critique before build

The previous B repeated performance, anniversary timing, uncertainty and opportunity gaps across multiple cards. Delete the center anniversary block, duplicated stat table, lengthy visible claim boundaries and the second retrospective explanation. Keep one event summary and one evidence reader. Short source assertions convey provenance; the original assertion, dates, scope and identifiers remain inspectable in disclosures. The Media tab has one truthful incomplete state. Connections remain a secondary source-supported summary, with no inferred roster or teammate relationship.

Do not turn branded dark mode into a new landing page. Event type remains a normal workspace subheading, the athlete identity stays strongest, and blue is limited to evidence selection/links while gold belongs to root's selected subject/navigation.

Bills–Bears September 7, 2014: three tackles, one assist, four combined tackles and nine source-confirmed defensive plays; Bills 23, Bears 20. Neither event has verified Moment media. Source documents establish facts, not cleared marketing assets. No Getty source, inferred ownership, contributor or earnings relationship is introduced.
