# Separate Bio and Life Story - October 19, 2026

## Objective and scope
Give the preview Locker builder independent short Bio and longer Life Story fields. The existing Locker Bio view shows only Bio; its Story filter shows only Life Story. Product reference: user request and docs/product/BLTZ_PRODUCT_DOCTRINE.md. Preserve existing bios, manual edits, unrelated content, permissions and old-editor compatibility. No canonical identity migration or new routes.

## Completion report
1. **Summary:** Renamed the visible builder Biography label to Bio (short overview), retaining its existing 4,000-character bound for compatibility and reducing the editor to four rows. Added a separate 12-row Life story editor with a 20,000-character bound, guidance and count. Removed the shared Locker’s Bio fallback and generated Early Life/College/Professional paragraphs from Story. Empty Story now says Life story has not been added yet. Existing bios remain untouched; new Story starts empty. Paragraph breaks are retained. Scraper merges preserve Story even when it is intentionally blank, and continue to preserve an entered Bio.
2. **Files changed:** app/admin/preview-lockers/PreviewLockerForm.tsx; app/player/[slug]/LockerView.tsx; app/api/preview-lockers/route.ts and [id]/route.ts; lib/preview-lockers/validation.ts, types.ts, server.ts, mapper.ts and merge-suggestions.ts; types/preview-lockers.generated.ts; tests/preview-lockers/bio-story.test.tsx and api.test.ts; scripts/verify-preview-story.mjs; migration below; this report. POST /api/preview-lockers and PATCH /api/preview-lockers/[id] accept/save them. /preview-lockers/[slug] receives saved Story. The shared /player/[slug] renderer also stops deriving Story from Bio, but this task does not add canonical Story authoring/storage. No new routes.

## Product direction
Strengthens the accuracy and persistence of athlete-authored career context separately from a brief identity introduction, useful across affiliations and retirement. No new canonical relationships or Moment/Value Graph entities, no mature third-party workflow duplication and no drift toward general media management.

## React and database review
Reuses existing form state, save handlers, shared Locker rendering, access gates and plain-text escaping. No new effect or event listener. Inputs have labels/help, existing focus styling and responsive layout. Manual Story is explicitly excluded from automated merge filling, while old clients cannot erase fields they omit.
